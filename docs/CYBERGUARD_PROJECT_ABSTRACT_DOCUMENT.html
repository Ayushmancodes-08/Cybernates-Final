1. Executive Abstract OVERVIEW

Modern state-level digital platforms, educational portals, and e-governance systems face an unprecedented security crisis caused by the convergence of automated cyber reconnaissance and Generative AI deception. Attackers breach state infrastructure by exploiting the blindspot between machine firewalls and human cognitive trust. CYBERGUARD, developed by Team Cybernates, is an autonomous, multi-model AI cyber defense and adaptive firewall platform built on the core paradigm: "AI Advises, Deterministic Policy Enforces." CyberGuard deploys an ensemble of 6 specialized neural models (fine-tuned RoBERTa for NLP/DLT smishing, RawNet2 spectral CNN for voice clone deepfakes, ResNet50 for quishing/video seams, Flow Isolation Forests for network anomaly detection, a Heterogeneous Relational Graph Attention Network (RGAT GNN) for multi-hop campaign correlation, and Qwen 2.5 Instruct for explainable SOC triage). When threat scores exceed 85/100, our deterministic policy engine synthesizes ephemeral nftables rules directly into the Linux kernel via low-level Netlink sockets (AF_NETLINK) with automatic Time-to-Live (TTL) expiration counters and immutable cryptographic receipts. CyberGuard reduces the industry-average Mean Time to Respond (MTTR) from 45 minutes to < 50 milliseconds (a 95%+ reduction), provides 0% packet leakage, zero permanent rule bloat, and full compliance with Indian CERT-In cybersecurity directives.

2. Problem Title & Description THE DUAL-PLANE CRISIS

Problem Statement 9: CYBERGUARD — AI-Powered Cyber Threat, Phishing & Digital Impersonation Detection and Response System. Critical state platforms (such as Odisha Knowledge Corporation Limited) operate under a dual-plane threat model that bypasses traditional single-plane defenses:

A. Technical Attack Plane (L3/L4/L7)

Adversaries deploy automated botnets for distributed TCP SYN sweeps, SSH/RDP brute-force spraying, geo-velocity impossible travel (>800 km/h), and API token exfiltration targeting core portal gateways.

B. Human Attack Plane (Social / L7)

Adversaries weaponize neural vocoders (<3s sample) to clone leadership voices in VoIP calls, send spoofed TRAI DLT SMS (OKCL-GOV), plant physical scholarship QR posters, and host zero-day look-alike portals.

⚠️ The Fatal Correlation Void: Existing defenses operate in isolated silos. When an attacker sends a spoofed SMS, clones an executive's voice, and sweeps gateway ports simultaneously, traditional systems inspect them as three harmless, unrelated events—allowing coordinated APT campaigns to penetrate state systems undetected.

5. Proposed Solution: CYBERGUARD & Why It's Better CORE INNOVATION
⚖️ Core Architectural Principle: "AI Advises, Deterministic Policy Enforces" — Probabilistic AI extracts features and calculates continuous risk vectors (0–100). Only our deterministic policy engine evaluates mathematical gates, synthesizes ephemeral rules, and verifies Linux kernel receipts.

1. Autonomous Adaptive Firewall (Netlink)

Synthesizes ephemeral drop rules in <11ms for threats ≥85 risk. Injects directly into kernel nftables via AF_NETLINK sockets with automatic TTL countdown (3600s) and cryptographic APPLIED_VERIFIED receipts.

2. Multimodal Threat Lab (Audio/Video/QR)

• Audio: 128-band Mel-Spectrogram detects vocoder cutoffs (>8.15kHz) and pitch flatlines.
• Video: DCT & Grad-CAM saliency reveals seams.
• Quishing: Decodes QR codes & homoglyphs.

3. Heterogeneous Attack Graph (RGAT GNN)

Models enterprise threats as a 6-node graph: Actor ↔ DLT Header ↔ SMS ↔ Domain ↔ IP ↔ User. Uncovers hidden multi-hop APT infrastructure and collapses campaigns at the root C2 node.

4. Contextual AI SOC Copilot (Qwen 2.5)

Natural language triage grounded strictly in telemetric facts, generating MITRE ATT&CK mapping (T1566, T1110, T1078) with zero-hallucination guardrails and automated response playbooks.
